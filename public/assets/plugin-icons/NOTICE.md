# Service brand icons

These marks identify integrations, not an affiliation or endorsement. Trademarks
remain the property of their respective owners.

Notion, GitHub, Slack, Google Drive, Linear, Jira, Microsoft Teams and Dropbox
SVGs are vendored from [gilbarbara/logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/tree/main/logos)
(CC0-1.0 collection), retrieved 2026-09-30. Original filenames: `notion-icon.svg`,
`github-icon.svg`, `slack-icon.svg`, `google-drive-2020.svg`, `linear-icon.svg`,
`jira.svg`, `microsoft-teams.svg`, `dropbox.svg`.

The Outlook mark is from [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/icons/microsoftoutlook.svg)
(CC0-1.0 collection); its fill is set to Outlook blue. Calendar adds a separate
UI badge to distinguish the two Outlook integrations.

The SVGs are static local image assets. Opening the catalog does not contact
an icon CDN or a service provider. XML declarations were removed; brand geometry
was preserved. [CC0-1.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
